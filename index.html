⌂ Ten Mile Point Resort · night 2 of 2
Practical notes & alternatives
  • Optional swap: Cup and Saucer in the morning, Bridal Veil Falls in the afternoon; omit Gore Bay. Allow 2½–3 hours for hiking.
  • Cup and Saucer is rugged, with cliff edges and a reported ladder section requiring dogs to be carried. Only choose it if comfortable carrying Pumpkin safely; skip the adventure add-on.
  • Rain plan: shorten the falls visit, keep waterfront stops flexible, and return to the cabin earlier.
  • Idyll Glen is a photo stop, not Pumpkin’s walk. The municipality lists designated parking but bans dogs on Lake Mindemoya beaches; parking-area dog rules and the view from the car remain unverified.
  • September 29 sunset is approximately 7:15 p.m. in Central Manitoulin. Skip the sunset stop if weather or access is unsuitable.
  • Spring Bay is a routing waypoint, not an extra scheduled attraction. This southern route reduces backtracking; the final local approach to Idyll Glen and the resort may still share roads.
  • Providence Bay is a 30-minute stop, not a second long hike. If running more than 20 minutes late, shorten Gore Bay or Providence Bay; keep the sunset arrival buffer.
  • East Bluff Lookout is an optional Gore Bay photo stop within the existing harbour visit; shorten harbour sightseeing if adding it, and keep the afternoon walk and sunset timing.
